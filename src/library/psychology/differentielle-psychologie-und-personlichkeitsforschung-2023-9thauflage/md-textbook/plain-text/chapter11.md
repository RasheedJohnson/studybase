## Chapter opening (DE)

Kapitel 11 behandelt Selbst und Persönlichkeit über McAdams' Drei-Schichten-Modell: Schicht 1 (Akteur: Traits und Selbstregulation inkl. Belohnungsaufschub, Impulsivität, Gewissenhaftigkeit und Verträglichkeit), Schicht 2 (Agent: motivationale Agenda, Motive, Regulationsfokus, Werte, Kontrollüberzeugungen, Selbstwirksamkeit, Selbstkonzept) und Schicht 3 (Autor: narrative Identität, Messung, Validität, Struktur, Generativität und Erlösungsgeschichten).

## Chapter opening (EN)

Chapter 11 treats self and personality through McAdams's three-layer model: Layer 1 (actor: traits and self-regulation including delay of gratification, impulsivity, Conscientiousness and Agreeableness), Layer 2 (agent: motivational agenda, motives, regulatory focus, values, locus of control, self-efficacy, self-concept), and Layer 3 (author: narrative identity, measurement, validity, structure, generativity and redemption stories).

## Summary notes

Textbook Zusammenfassungen exist for Abschnitte 11.1, 11.2, 11.3, and 11.4. Concept cards prioritize chapter-specific self constructs and the self-personality link within McAdams's layers. Basics already covered in Chapters 1-10 (Big Five structure labels, PEN/NEO impulsivity placement, BIS/BAS systems, Bandura observational learning of aggression, Erikson's full stage list, general reliability/validity vocabulary, self-monitoring/self-awareness as prediction moderators) are not re-added unless Kapitel 11 adds a distinct self-regulation, expectancy, self-concept, or narrative-identity finding.
