## Chapter opening (DE)

Wie gut können Eigenschaften zukünftiges Verhalten vorhersagen, und welche Situationsinformationen braucht man dazu? Kapitel 9 stellt Modellvorstellungen (Personismus, Situationismus, Interaktionismus, Dispositionismus) und methodische sowie inhaltliche Wege zur Verbesserung der Verhaltensvorhersage vor.

## Chapter opening (EN)

How well can traits predict future behavior, and what situational information is required? Chapter 9 presents model frames (personism, situationism, interactionism, dispositionism) and methodological plus substantive routes to improve behavioral prediction.

## Summary notes

Textbook Zusammenfassungen exist for Abschnitte 9.1 and 9.2. Concept cards prioritize chapter-specific prediction models, the consistency debate, aggregation tactics, situation moderators, and predictability moderators. Basics already covered in Chapters 1-8 (trait prediction definition, cross-situational consistency definition, Conley stability, social desirability as response bias, general aggregation/reliability) are not re-added.
