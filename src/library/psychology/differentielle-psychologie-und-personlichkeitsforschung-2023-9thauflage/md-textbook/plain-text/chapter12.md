## Chapter opening (DE)

Kapitel 12 stellt Verhaltensgenetik vor: quantitative Genetik (Zwillings- und Adoptionsdesigns, Varianzzerlegung, ACE-Modell, rGE, GxE), Befunde zu Intelligenz und Persönlichkeit (Turkheimers Gesetze, altersabhängige Erblichkeit, Scarr-Rowe-Hypothese), Molekulargenetik (GWAS, SNP-Heritabilität, PGS, indirekte genetische Effekte), Epigenetik (Methylierung, epigenetische Clocks) sowie Umweltmaße (HOME, Geschwisterreihe, Erziehung).

## Chapter opening (EN)

Chapter 12 presents behavioral genetics: quantitative genetics (twin and adoption designs, variance decomposition, ACE model, rGE, GxE), findings on intelligence and personality (Turkheimer's laws, age-dependent heritability, Scarr-Rowe hypothesis), molecular genetics (GWAS, SNP heritability, PGS, indirect genetic effects), epigenetics (methylation, epigenetic clocks), and environmental measures (HOME, birth order, parenting).
