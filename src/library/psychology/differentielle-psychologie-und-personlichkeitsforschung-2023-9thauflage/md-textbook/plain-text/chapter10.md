## Chapter opening (DE)

Kapitel 10 behandelt Emotion und Persönlichkeit über drei große Blöcke: Freuds Psychoanalyse und Neoanalyse als historisch einflussreiche, aber empirisch schwer prüfbare Persönlichkeitstheorie; die differentialpsychologische Forschung zu Ängstlichkeit (Messung, Kognition, Leistung, Biologie, Geschlecht); und Aggression bzw. Aggressivität (Definition, Trieb/Katharsis, Lernen, Trait-Maße, Geschlechtsunterschiede).

## Chapter opening (EN)

Chapter 10 treats emotion and personality in three large blocks: Freud's psychoanalysis and neoanalysis as historically influential but empirically hard-to-test personality theory; differential research on trait anxiety (measurement, cognition, performance, biology, sex); and aggression or aggressiveness (definition, drive/catharsis, learning, trait measures, sex differences).

## Summary notes

Textbook Zusammenfassungen exist for Abschnitte 10.1, 10.2, and 10.3. Concept cards prioritize chapter-specific psychoanalytic structures and dynamics, trait-anxiety differentials (STAI, situation and response specificity, processing efficiency, amygdala network), and aggression theory/measurement. Basics already covered in Chapters 1-9 (PEN/Big Five structure labels, Gray anxiety-vs-fear and BIS/BAS systems, PANAS as general affective style, ARAS/limbic Neuroticism biology, emotional intelligence, general LST/reliability vocabulary) are not re-added unless Kapitel 10 adds a distinct anxiety- or aggression-specific finding.
