## Core concept clusters

### 8.1 / 8.1.1-8.1.7
Deductive vs inductive approaches; differential pitfalls of EEG, MRI/fMRI, pharmacology (phenomenon-centered vs differential-response / Challenge tests), peripheral physiology (law of initial values), candidate genes vs GWAS and population stratification, animal transfer limits, emerging stimulation and ambulatory methods.

### 8.2 / 8.2.1-8.2.3
ARAS, optimal arousal and hedonischer Tonus; Extraversion predictions (sensory, vigilance, EEG, drug postulate); limbic Aktivation for Neuroticism; continuity/diathesis framing for Psychoticism biology; conceptual limits (E-N independence, unidimensional arousal).

### 8.3 / 8.3.1-8.3.4
RST original vs revised; BAS, BIS, FFS/FFFS; anxiety vs fear; Gray's rotation of E/N into anxiety and impulsivity; E as BIS-BAS balance and N as sum; mixed empirical support.

### 8.4 / 8.4.1-8.4.3
Biosocial theory; NS-dopamine, HA-serotonin, RD-norepinephrine; TPQ; Challenge tests; 5-HTTLPR failure; TCI expansion and factorial critique.

### 8.5 / 8.5.1-8.5.2
DeYoung CB5T cybernetics, Plasticity/Stability metatraits, aspects; dopamine/serotonin claims; Zuckerman multi-level model; Sensation Seeking and SSS-V; MAO; Augmenting-Reducing.

### 8.6 / 8.6.1-8.6.2
Depue BFS and agentic Extraversion; Constraint/serotonin; Davidson frontal asymmetry and affective style; PANAS; valence-motivation confound.

### 8.7 / 8.7.1-8.7.2
Exploratory BWAS/GWAS; tiny effects and sample-size needs; missing heritability; neuropsychometrics, abstraction-level, and coherent-reduction problems.
