## Core concept clusters

### 5.1 / 5.1.1 / 5.1.2
Causal process explanation of intelligence differences; Sternberg componential approach and its limits; mental speed via ECTs (inspection time, Hick, Sternberg scan, Posner); working memory capacity; Jensen speed-capacity synthesis.

### 5.2
P-FIT network; ERP latency findings; neural efficiency; myelinization / pruning / plasticity accounts; brain volume-IQ link; speed as correlate not proven cause.

### 5.3.1-5.3.7
Other-rating validity; disability continuum and grades; giftedness; complex problem solving methodology; learning (explicit/implicit); school and job prediction; g dominance; Terman study; SES.

### 5.4 / 5.5
Academic vs practical intelligence; tacit knowledge and simulations; Gardner TMI and critique; social, successful, and emotional intelligence (ability vs trait EI); limited incremental validity.
