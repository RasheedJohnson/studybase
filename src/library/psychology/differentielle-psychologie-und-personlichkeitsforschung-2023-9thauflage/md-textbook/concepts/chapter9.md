## Core concept clusters

### 9.1.1
Personism absolute vs relative consistency equations; Mischel personality coefficient (~0.30); functional equivalence; BESD; classical low-consistency studies; conditions for consistency; ability vs personality prediction asymmetry.

### 9.1.2
Situationism model; actor-observer bias; Kenrick and Funder rebuttal of situationist arguments; both person and situation matter for variance.

### 9.1.3
Interactionism with Person x Situation term; statistical interactionism critique; persons select and filter situations.

### 9.1.4
Dispositions as if-then sets; dispositionism prediction; coherence vs cross-situational consistency; CAPS/CAUs; stable individual situation-behavior profiles.

### 9.2.1-9.2.3
Epstein aggregation over occasions; multiple-act criteria; aggregation across situations (gain vs masking); shared psychological situation elements.

### 9.2.4-9.2.7
Strong vs weak situations; Buss conditions; trait relevance; visibility; centrality/appropriateness; self-focused attention (mirror) and response-time controls.

### 9.2.8
Predictability moderators: social desirability, self-monitoring, private/public self-awareness, self-rated consistency, traitedness/metatraits; person-centered approaches.
