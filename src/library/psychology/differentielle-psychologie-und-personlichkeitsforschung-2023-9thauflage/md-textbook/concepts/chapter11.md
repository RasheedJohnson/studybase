## Core concept clusters

### 11.1
Three-layer model; actor / agent / author perspectives; developmental timing (temperament and traits early; goals and values from middle childhood; narrative identity from late teens / young adulthood); McAdams layered definition of personality.

### 11.2.1-11.2.3
Self-regulation vs self-control; impulsivity as impulse strength plus weak inhibition; UPPS factors; delay of gratification (Mischel) vs delay discounting; situational and model influences; effortful control as temperament root of Conscientiousness and Agreeableness; C/A as self-regulation competence for relationships, work, health and longevity.

### 11.3.1-11.3.4
Motivational agenda; extrinsic vs intrinsic motivation; achievement, power, and affiliation motives (Murray / McClelland / PSE); Higgins promotion vs prevention focus; Haidt moral foundations as value anchors; Rotter expectancy x reinforcement value and internal vs external LOC; Bandura self-efficacy vs outcome expectancy and domain specificity; James I/Me; self-concept as descriptive-evaluative knowledge system; Shavelson hierarchy and SDQ; reality congruence of academic vs social self-concept.

### 11.4.1-11.4.5
Narrative identity definition and developmental precursors; life-story interview; agency, communion, redemption; incremental validity over Big Five; three-factor structure (McLean et al.); generativity and redemption stories for meaning.
