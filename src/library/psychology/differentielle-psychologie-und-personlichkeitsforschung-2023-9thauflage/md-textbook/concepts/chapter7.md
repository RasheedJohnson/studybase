## Core concept clusters

### 7.1 / 7.1.1-7.1.2
No general personality g; Hippocratic temperaments; Wundt/Eysenck dimensional remapping onto Extraversion and Emotional Stability-Lability; Caspi childhood types; Kretschmer constitution types and temperament correlates; age confound; mixed types push toward dimensional models.

### 7.2 / 7.2.1-7.2.5
Source vs surface traits; psycholexical Allport-Odbert path; L-, Q-, and T-data; 12 L-factors and 16 PF; hierarchy with preferred primary level; objective tests; replication and overextraction critiques.

### 7.3 / 7.3.1-7.3.4
Habits-traits-types hierarchy; PEN / Giant Three; Extraversion, Neuroticism, Psychoticism continuum; EPQ-R; social-behavior applications; later verdict that E and N hold while P is misspecified.

### 7.4 / 7.4.1-7.4.4
Lexical hypothesis; Tupes and Christal / Goldberg Big Five; Norman labels including Culture; Costa and McCrae N-E-O-A-C with domains and facets; NEO instruments; Zuckerman Alternative Five; HEXACO Honesty-Humility; Eysenck-Costa debate; etic vs emic cross-cultural evidence.

### 7.5 / 7.5.1-7.5.2
Differential vs absolute stability for personality; Conley stability hierarchy; Big Five mean-level lifespan trajectories; investment theory of identity development.

### 7.6
Feingold meta-analysis; sex-difference pattern on Big Five facets; dominance vs submissiveness framing.
